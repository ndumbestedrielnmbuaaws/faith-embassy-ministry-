Who we are

A house of prayer for all nations

Faith Embassy Ministry International is a Bible-believing, Holy Ghost-filled church. Like an embassy represents its home country in a foreign land, we exist to represent heaven here on earth: its love, its truth and its power.

Rooted in Makanga, Muyuka, in the South West Region of Cameroon, our family brings together people of many languages and cultures, united by one Lord, one faith and one baptism. Whether you are seeking God for the first time or looking for a church home, there is a place for you here.

And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.Acts 2:4

Our mission

To win souls for Christ, raise disciples who live by faith, and send ambassadors of the Gospel to every nation.

Our vision

A Spirit-empowered international family transforming communities through prayer, the Word and acts of love.

Our values

Holiness, prayer, worship, faithfulness to Scripture, love for one another and generous service.

Leadership

Led by our General Overseer, Evangelist Isabel Elinge Godwill, and a team of ministers, elders and deacons who serve the church with humility.

Statement of faith

What we believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith and the full Gospel as preached by the apostles on the day of Pentecost.

  • James 5:14–15Divine healingProvided through the atonement of Christ and received by faith.
